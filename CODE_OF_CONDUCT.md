# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and abusive behavior are not
accepted.

Security findings must follow `SECURITY.md`; never post exploit details or credentials
in a public issue. Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions
that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect
the community.

For conduct concerns that cannot safely be discussed publicly, open a minimal issue
requesting a private contact channel without including sensitive details.
